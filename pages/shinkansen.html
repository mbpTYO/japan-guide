How to Ride the Shinkansen

新幹線の乗り方

← All articles / 記事一覧へ

The Shinkansen (新幹線) is Japan's high-speed train network. The rides are frequent, remarkably punctual and easy once you know the order of things: choose a train, buy the right ticket, pass the gate, find your car, board, and leave through the gate with the same ticket or card. The only real trap is that two groups of companies run the network, so the booking site depends on where you are going.

新幹線は日本の高速鉄道です。列車を選び、切符を買い、改札を通り、号車を探して乗り、同じ切符・カードで出る。この順番を押さえれば、乗るのはとても簡単です。ただし会社が2グループに分かれるので、予約サイトが目的地によって違います。

1. Two networks, two booking sites 2つのグループと予約サイト

JR East runs the Tohoku, Hokkaido, Joetsu, Hokuriku, Yamagata and Akita Shinkansen. Use JR-EAST Train Reservation (also known as Eki-net) for these trains. Its ticketless Shinkansen e-ticket links your booking to a Suica, PASMO or similar IC card; you then tap the card at the gate. The Welcome Suica Mobile app also offers “Touch de Go! Shinkansen”, which lets you tap into non-reserved seats on many JR East routes without booking — but not on the all-reserved Hayabusa, Hayate, Komachi or Tsubasa.

JR Central, JR West and JR Kyushu run the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and West Kyushu Shinkansen. Use Smart EX (website or app) for these. You can travel with a QR ticket — scan it at the gate and a small “Seat Information” slip prints out — or register an IC card such as Suica, TOICA, ICOCA or SUGOCA and just tap it. With an IC card, the Shinkansen fare is charged to the credit card you registered, not deducted from the card's balance.

You can always buy at the station instead: the Midori-no-Madoguchi ticket office, a ticket machine or a reserved-seat machine. Machines have multilingual menus and are a useful backup if your credit card is refused online. A Japan Rail Pass alone does not cover the Nozomi; a separate Nozomi option ticket is needed.

東日本（東北・北海道・上越・北陸・山形・秋田）は「JR東日本ネット予約（えきねっと）」、東海・山陽・九州・西九州は「スマートEX」が基本です。QRチケットかICカード登録で切符なしに乗れます。駅の「みどりの窓口」や券売機でも買えます。JRパスだけではのぞみには乗れません。

2. What you are actually buying 切符のしくみ

A Shinkansen fare has two parts: a basic fare based on distance, and a limited express charge based on the seat type. They may come as one ticket or as two pieces of paper. Two tickets do not mean you were charged twice.

Seat typeWhat it means
Reserved (指定席, shiteiseki)A fixed train, car and seat. Best for groups, holidays and a fixed plan. A few hundred yen more than non-reserved.
Non-reserved (自由席, jiyuseki)Any non-reserved car on any train that day, first come, first served. If it is full, you stand. Nozomi has no non-reserved cars during peak periods.
Green Car (グリーン車)Wider seats and more room, with a separate surcharge.

Some trains are all reserved: Hayabusa, Hayate, Komachi, Tsubasa and Kagayaki have no non-reserved cars, so you must book a seat. On the Tokaido line, typical non-reserved cars are Cars 1–2 on Nozomi, 1–5 on Hikari and 1–6 plus 13–16 on Kodama, but this can change — check the platform sign.

運賃（距離に応じる）と特急料金（座席の種類に応じる）の2つが切符の正体です。指定席は列車・号車・席が固定。自由席は先着順で、混雑時は立ちます。はやぶさ等は全車指定席です。

3. Through the gate 改札の通り方

Read the departure board first. On the Tokaido line the train name is colour-coded: yellow = Nozomi (fastest), red = Hikari, blue = Kodama (stops most). Boards switch between Japanese and English, and show the departure time, destination and platform.

  1. Paper tickets: if you have two tickets (basic fare + limited express), stack them and insert both together in one go. Take them from the gate — you need them to leave.
  2. Ticket plus IC card: insert the paper ticket first, then tap the card.
  3. QR ticket: find a gate with a QR scanner, hold up your phone, and take the “Seat Information” slip that prints. It is a seat reminder for the crew, not a ticket — do not insert it in the exit gate.
  4. Registered IC card or e-ticket: just tap the same card used at booking. If you entered through a conventional-line gate (for example Tokyo Station's Marunouchi side), tap at both the conventional gate and the Shinkansen gate.

発車標はのぞみ＝黄色、ひかり＝赤、こだま＝青。紙の切符が2枚なら重ねて一度に入れ、必ず受け取ります。QRなら改札で出た「座席情報」の紙を保管し、出口の改札には入れないでください。

4. On the platform, and your luggage ホームと荷物のルール

Go to the platform shown on the board and find the mark for your car number. Lines are painted on the platform edge so you can queue in the right place before the train arrives; doors stay open only briefly. Once on board, match the car and seat number on your ticket or slip.

Luggage rules depend on size, measured as the sum of the three sides (length + width + height):

SizeWhat to do
Up to 160 cmUse the overhead rack, the space at your feet or the deck area. No reservation needed.
Over 160 cm and up to 250 cmOn the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and West Kyushu lines, reserve an oversized baggage seat (特大荷物スペースつき座席) when you buy your ticket. It is the last seat of a reserved car, costs the same as a normal reserved seat, and has no extra fee. Without a reservation you may be charged a ¥1,000 handling fee on board.
Over 250 cmCannot be brought on board at all.

Strollers, wheelchairs, musical instruments and sports equipment are exempt from the reservation rule regardless of size. On JR East Shinkansen there are no dedicated oversized-baggage seats; use the deck luggage areas without a reservation. If in doubt, measure before you go — major stations have luggage measuring stands near the ticket office.

荷物は3辺の合計で決まります。160cm以下は荷棚・足元へ。160cm超〜250cmは東海道・山陽・九州・西九州で「特大荷物スペースつき座席」の予約が必要（追加料金なし、無予約なら1,000円）。250cm超は持ち込み不可。ベビーカーや楽器は対象外です。

5. On board 車内での過ごし方

  • Food: buy an ekiben (station bento) before boarding — ordinary cars on the Tokaido line do not have food service. Every seat has a fold-down table.
  • Quiet: texting and browsing are fine, but take voice calls to the deck between cars and keep videos silent.
  • No smoking anywhere on the train; use smoking rooms at stations.
  • Mt Fuji: on a westbound Tokaido train, seat E in ordinary class (D in Green) has the usual view — on a clear day.
  • Comfort: power outlets at Green Car seats and at ordinary window/front/back rows (every seat on newer trains), free Wi-Fi on Tokaido Shinkansen, and restrooms in odd-numbered cars on 16-car trains (Car 11 is accessible).
  • Keep your ticket or slip with you until you leave the station.

駅で駅弁を買ってから乗ります。通話はデッキへ、車内は禁煙。富士山側は普通車のE席、グリーン車のD席がおすすめです。

6. Getting off and exiting 降りるとき・出口

Stops can be short, so gather your things as you approach the station and check the announcements — your stop may not be the terminus. At the exit gate, insert your paper ticket (the machine keeps it) or tap the same QR code or IC card. If you are continuing onto a conventional line, follow the signs to the conventional-line transfer gate (在来線乗換改札); at some stations the conventional gates are only after the Shinkansen gates. With a paper ticket, take any ticket the transfer gate returns.

停車時間が短いので早めに準備します。出口改札で切符を入れるか、同じQR・ICカードをタッチします。在来線に乗り換える場合は「在来線乗換改札」の表示をたどってください。

8. If something goes wrong トラブル時は

  • Missed your train: with a QR or paper ticket you can ride the non-reserved section of a later train the same day on the same route. Discounted products may be tied to the booked train — check the rules of your ticket.
  • Delay or suspension: check the boards and English announcements. If you booked online, log in to change your travel; ask staff for a delay certificate if you need one.
  • Lost IC card or ticket: tell staff at the manned gate. With a smart EX booking you can collect a paper ticket at a machine using your pick-up code.
  • Gate will not open: step out of the queue and use the staffed gate rather than forcing it.

Quick checklist 確認リスト

  • Decide your region, then book on Smart EX or JR-EAST Train Reservation (or buy at the station).
  • Reserve a seat for a group, a holiday, or luggage over 160 cm.
  • Measure your bags: over 160 cm needs a baggage seat, over 250 cm is not allowed.
  • At the gate, stack both paper tickets or scan/tap; take the Seat Information slip.
  • Check platform, car and seat; keep your ticket until you exit.
  • Follow the transfer signs if you are continuing onto a conventional line.
